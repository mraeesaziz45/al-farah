What We Do

Comprehensive care. Clear answers.

Al-Farah brings general consultation, diagnostics, and specialist referral together, so patients spend less time coordinating care and more time understanding it.

01 / Diagnostics

Radiology & Imaging

Diagnostic imaging forms the basis of many clinical decisions, and Al-Farah treats it as a service in its own right rather than an afterthought to consultation.

Every study is reviewed by a qualified radiologist before results are shared, with findings explained in plain terms to the referring physician and, where appropriate, the patient directly.

  • Imaging consultation
  • Diagnostic imaging
  • Specialist review
  • Patient guidance

02 / Primary Care

General Consultation

Most patients begin here. A general consultation covers history, assessment, and a considered plan — whether that means treatment, monitoring, or referral to a specialist.

Appointments are scheduled with enough time to ask questions and leave with a clear understanding of next steps.

  • Initial assessment
  • Medical history review
  • Treatment planning
  • Referral guidance

03 / Diagnostics

Laboratory Diagnostics

Standard laboratory testing is processed with attentive turnaround, and results are explained rather than simply handed over as figures on a page.

Where results indicate the need for further evaluation, the laboratory team coordinates directly with the referring physician.

  • Sample collection
  • Standard laboratory testing
  • Result explanation
  • Physician coordination

04 / Referral Care

Specialist Consultation

Some cases need focused, ongoing attention beyond general practice. Al-Farah maintains referral access to specialist practitioners for these situations.

The referring physician remains involved throughout, so specialist input is integrated into the patient's broader care rather than handled in isolation.

  • Specialist referral
  • Case coordination
  • Ongoing follow-up
  • Shared records
Request an Appointment

05 / Screening

Preventive Care

Preventive care is oriented around early detection rather than late intervention — routine screening intended to catch issues while they remain manageable.

Screening schedules are discussed individually, based on age, history, and risk factors rather than a one-size-fits-all calendar.

  • Routine screening
  • Risk assessment
  • Personalized scheduling
  • Follow-up planning

06 / Coordination

Patient Support

Administrative and follow-up support sits behind every clinical service — scheduling, records requests, and coordination between departments.

The aim is straightforward: patients should never be left uncertain about what happens next or who to contact.

  • Appointment scheduling
  • Records coordination
  • Follow-up reminders
  • General inquiries
